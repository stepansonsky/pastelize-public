# Pastelize Privacy Policy
Last Updated: February 20, 2026

I hold myself to high ethical and moral standards. Pastelize executes ONLY the command to set saturation through the ADB protocol and nothing else. To be fully transparent, I will be open-sourcing the core of the app soon.

This policy explains how I handle your data — which, in short, is as little as possible.

## Data Collection and Usage
Pastelize does not collect personal information (such as your name, email, or contact list). I only collect non-personally identifiable technical data for the following purposes:

### Crash Logs
If the app crashes, I receive technical details about the error so I can fix it.

### Device Compatibility
I collect basic device information (e.g., brand, model and OS version) to ensure the app performs well on your specific hardware.

## Third-Party Services
We use industry-standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics and Google Firebase Crashlytics) to process the technical data mentioned above. These services do not receive any information that could be used to identify you personally.

## Consent
By using Pastelize, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact me using Google Play Store.
